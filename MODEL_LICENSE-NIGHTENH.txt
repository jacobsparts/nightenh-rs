The night-enhancement generator this engine reimplements, the checkpoint tensors
it reads, and the upstream Python that `tools/reference.py` was transcribed from
are the work of:

    Yeying Jin, Wenhan Yang, Robby T. Tan
    "Unsupervised Night Image Enhancement: When Layer Decomposition Meets
     Light-Effects Suppression"
    European Conference on Computer Vision (ECCV) 2022
    https://github.com/jinyeying/night-enhancement

WHAT THIS FILE COVERS, and what it does not:
  * `nightenh-lol.safetensors` and `nightenh-delighteffects.safetensors` are
    FORMAT CONVERSIONS of the upstream `LOL_params_0900000.pt` and
    `delighteffects_params_0600000.pt` checkpoints. The weights are the authors'
    work and remain under the terms above; they are NOT covered by this
    repository's MIT licence.
  * `tools/reference.py` is a transcription of the upstream `networks.py`
    generator and `tools/convert.py` reads the upstream checkpoints' containers:
    both are derived works under the same terms, NOT under this repository's MIT
    licence.
  * `src/`, `cuda/`, `build.rs` and `Cargo.toml` are an independent
    reimplementation of the architecture and are MIT licensed; see LICENSE.
    Running them on the converted checkpoints does not make them derivative
    works of the upstream code.
  * The upstream `.pt` files themselves are NOT redistributed here.
